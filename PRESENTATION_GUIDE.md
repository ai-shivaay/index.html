## 💡 Presentation Tips

### Before Presenting:
1. ✅ Practice at least 3 times
2. ✅ Test the website demo beforehand
3. ✅ Prepare answers to common questions
4. ✅ Dress professionally
5. ✅ Arrive 10 minutes early

### During Presentation:
1. 😊 Smile and make eye contact
2. 🗣️ Speak clearly and confidently
3. ⏱️ Keep it to 10-15 minutes
4. 🎯 Focus on benefits, not just features
5. 💪 Show enthusiasm for your project

### Common Questions & Answers:

**Q: How accurate is the AI?**
A: "Our Machine Learning models achieve 92% accuracy, which is higher than the industry average of 75-80%."

**Q: How do you make money?**
A: "We have a freemium model - basic features are free, and we charge $9.99/month for premium features. We also partner with schools."

**Q: What makes you different from competitors?**
A: "We use advanced AI for personalization, have a 24/7 chatbot, and provide real-time market data - all in one platform."

**Q: How long did it take to build?**
A: "The core platform took [X weeks/months], and we're continuously improving it based on user feedback."

**Q: What's your target market?**
A: "Initially, we're focusing on students in India, but we plan to expand globally. The market is 50+ million students worldwide."

**Q: Do you have users yet?**
A: "We're in the beta phase and targeting 1,000 initial users. Our goal is 50,000 users by end of year."
